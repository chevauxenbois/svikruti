# LendFlow privacy notice

We collect your name and date of birth (identity), email and phone number (contact
details), Aadhaar and PAN (government identifiers used for KYC), and payment details
such as your UPI ID (financial information) to assess loan applications and collect
repayments.

We share data with service providers acting as our processors: Razorpay for payments
and MSG91 for SMS reminders.

We retain application data for eight years after loan closure as required by law, then delete it.
You can access, correct or erase your data, nominate another person, or withdraw consent at any
time by writing to privacy@lendflow.example. Complaints go to our Grievance Officer at the same address.
